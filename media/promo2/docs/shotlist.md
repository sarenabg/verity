# Verity promo (narrated): script and shot list
About 44 s · 16:9 (primary) and 9:16 · music: punchy synth house at 122 bpm, ducked under the voice
Voice: warm, confident, clear English narrator (ElevenLabs), with one clip per line so each scene is cut to its line.

## Narration script (≈100 words)

| # | line (spoken) | on-screen words (kinetic type) |
|---|---------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | "Encyclopedias tell you what's true. But never how sure anyone is." | **what's true** … **how sure?** |
| 2 | "Meet Verity: the encyclopedia where every fact has a price." | **Verity** · **every fact has a price** |
| 3 | "Think George Mallory reached the top of Everest first, in 1924? Put money on it." | **14%** |
| 4 | "As people bet, the odds move, and the AI rewrites the page to match." | **14% → 37%** · **rewritten** |
| 5 | "Pay with any coin you already own." | **any coin** |
| 6 | "When a fact can be checked, an AI judge settles it. Disagree? Three AIs vote." | **False ✓** · **3 AIs** |
| 7 | "Facts nobody can prove yet stay open. Honestly." | **still open** |
| 8 | "Verity. The encyclopedia that knows how sure it is. Built on NEAR." | end card |

## Shots (each runs as long as its line, plus a beat of breathing room)

| # | shot | motion (dynamic, real screens brought to life) |
|---|------|-----------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | A real article paragraph flies in at an angle; the words "what's true" punch in big, then a giant **?** drops and the paragraph tilts away |
| 2 | Brand | Logo check-mark draws; "Verity" slams in letter by letter; three real article cards fan out behind it with parallax |
| 3 | The claim | Camera dives into the Mallory sentence (orange underline draws itself); a huge **14%** counter pops out of the page; a cursor glides to the betting card and taps **$100** |
| 4 | The rewrite | Number counter rolls **14 → 37%**; a yellow highlight sweeps the sentence and the real rewritten text swaps in; the chart line on the card draws upward |
| 5 | Any coin | Real coin logos (BTC, ETH, SOL, USDC…) rain in on the beat and snap into the real coin picker; the live quote card flips up |
| 6 | The judge | The judge card slides up; a **Settled: False** stamp slams with a shake; then three vote dots pop one per beat with a "3 AIs" label |
| 7 | Open questions | The "Can't settle yet" card floats in, slow push; the word **open** stays lit |
| 8 | End card | Logo + "Verity" + tagline + site address + "Built on NEAR"; holds 2 s |

Tall 9:16: big type stacks above the screen, and screens are cropped to the part being talked about.
