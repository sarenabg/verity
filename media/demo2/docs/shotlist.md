# Verity walkthrough (narrated): script and shot list
About 90 s · 16:9 (primary) and 9:16 · music: warm lo-fi at 90 bpm, ducked under the voice
Voice: the same narrator as the promo, slightly slower.

## Narration script (≈210 words), one line per step

| # | line (spoken) | screen |
|---|---------------|--------|
| 1 | "This is Verity, an encyclopedia where every fact has a price." | Home page, slow glide |
| 2 | "Open any article. Each underlined sentence is a fact with its own market. Blue means people are sure. Orange means it's probably a myth." | Mount Everest; underlines light up one by one, a colour key pops in |
| 3 | "Did George Mallory reach the top of Everest in 1924? The market says fourteen percent. Let's bet a hundred dollars that he did." | Camera to the Mallory market card; cursor taps $100 and the Bet button |
| 4 | "Our bet pushes the odds past thirty percent, so the AI rewrites the sentence. It's now an open question." | Counter 14 → 37%; highlight sweep; the new sentence settles |
| 5 | "Every change is logged in the page history, and you can cash out at today's price, any time." | Page history line and the "Cash out" button glow |
| 6 | "No dollars handy? Pay with Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, or two hundred other coins. NEAR Intents changes them into dollars in seconds." | Coin picker → live ETH quote → three progress ticks |
| 7 | "Some facts can be checked. Can you see the Great Wall from space? Ask the AI judge. It says false, and when the waiting time is over, winners get paid." | Great Wall page → judge card → "Settled: False" stamp, page fact gets a ✓ |
| 8 | "Think the judge got it wrong? Dispute it, and three different AI models vote." | Votes pop in one by one |
| 9 | "If there's no proof yet, the judge says so, and the market stays open." | "Can't settle yet" card |
| 10 | "My bets shows your money, your bets, and how they're doing." | My bets page, numbers count up |
| 11 | "Ready for real money? Switch to Live. You bet in real dollars on NEAR, and your own wallet confirms every step." | Demo/Live picker → Live article → Connect wallet |
| 12 | "It works on your phone, too." | Phone frame; the bet sheet slides up |
| 13 | "Verity. The encyclopedia that knows how sure it is." | End card with the site address |

## Motion style
Each screen is a real capture, brought to life: the camera glides and pushes in to the part being talked about; a cursor moves and taps where the narrator says; numbers count; highlights sweep; key words from the line appear as large captions. Each step pushes to the next. A step number sits in the corner.
