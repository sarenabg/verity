# Verity

**The encyclopedia that knows how sure it is.**

Every fact in a Verity article has a prediction market behind it. People bet True or False, and the price is the world's confidence in the fact. An AI writes each page from those prices. Facts the market is sure of are stated plainly, shaky ones are called "disputed", and myths are called myths. When a bet pushes a fact across a line, the AI rewrites that sentence on the spot.

Built for the [NEAR Hackathon](https://nearhackathon.com) (Nov 2026). The idea is Elias's: *"An AI encyclopedia with prediction markets. Instead of community curation, every fact becomes a perpetual truth-seeking market (Augur-style). The official pages are generated by AI based on the current market understanding."*

There are two modes, switched with the pill in the header:

| | **Demo** (default) | **Live** |
|---|---|---|
| Money | $1,000 of play money in your browser | Real USDC on NEAR mainnet |
| Wallet | None | Any NEAR wallet (Meteor, HOT, MyNearWallet, Intear); you confirm every bet |
| Pay with any coin | Real NEAR Intents price; the transfer is simulated | Real NEAR Intents deposit address; coins arrive as USDC in your wallet |
| AI judge | Demo rulings (or NEAR AI with a key) | NEAR AI judge posts rulings to the contract |
| Label | Amber strip on every page: "Demo · Play money" | Blue "Live" pill |

## Run it (2 minutes, no keys needed)

**Quickest:** open the hosted demo at https://verity-pied-phi.vercel.app (Demo mode, play money, no wallet).

To run it yourself you need Node 20+.

```bash
cd web
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open http://localhost:3200. You start in **Demo** with $1,000 of play money.

### What to try

1. **Open [Mount Everest](http://localhost:3200/wiki/mount-everest).** Each underlined sentence is a fact with a market. The colour shows how sure the market is, and the small % is the price. The card on the right is the market for *"Did George Mallory reach the top in 1924?"* (14%).
2. **Bet $100 on True.** The price jumps to about 37%. The Mallory sentence crosses from "doubtful" to "disputed", so it is rewritten and highlighted. A new line appears under **Page history**.
3. **Click "Cash out".** You get your money back at today's price. Markets never close.
4. **Pay with any coin:** click *Pay with any coin*, then pick ETH. The amount comes from a live NEAR Intents quote, for example "$20 = 0.0091 ETH".
5. **Settle a fact:** on [Great Wall of China](http://localhost:3200/wiki/great-wall-of-china), bet on *False*, then click *Ask the AI judge to settle this*. The judge rules False, citing China's first astronaut. Click *Skip 48h (demo)* to pass the dispute window. The fact gets a ✓ and your winning shares pay $1 each. Or click *Dispute* instead, and three AI judges vote.
6. **Ask about an open question:** on Mount Everest, ask the judge about Mallory. It answers *"Can't settle yet"*, returns your deposit, and the market stays open.
7. **[My bets](http://localhost:3200/bets)** shows your balance, open bets with profit or loss, and activity. *Reset demo* starts over.
8. **Search** from the top bar (the magnifier on phones): try "tenzing" or "octopus dream". Arrow keys and Enter work.
9. **On a phone** (or a narrow window), tap any underlined fact. The market opens as a bottom sheet; after a bet that rewrites the page, the sheet closes so you see the new sentence.

### Live mode

Live is on the hosted site once the contract is deployed (see [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md)). Click **Demo** in the header, choose **Live**, and connect a NEAR wallet:
- **Bets** are `ft_transfer_call`s of USDC to the contract.
- **Cash out** calls `sell`; then **Withdraw to wallet**.
- **Ask the AI judge** posts a 1 USDC bond. The app's server runs the NEAR AI judge and posts the ruling on-chain using a key that can only call the two ruling functions.
- **Pay with any coin** gives a real one-time NEAR Intents address. The coins arrive as USDC in your own NEAR account, and then you confirm the bet.

When the site points at a testnet contract (`NEXT_PUBLIC_NEAR_NETWORK=testnet`), Live is labelled **Live · testnet** and says the dollars are test dollars.

`live-e2e` runs 15 checks. Settling is permanent, so each run picks checkable facts that are still open on the contract:
- bet → price moves → cash out;
- ask the judge (through the app's `/api/live/judge`) → ruling posted on-chain → finalize after the dispute window;
- dispute → 3-model panel settles it;
- redeem → withdraw to the wallet.

## How it works

| Piece | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Markets** | Each fact has a pool of True and False shares (a fixed-product market maker, as used by Gnosis and Polymarket). A winning share pays 1 USDC, so the True price is the probability. You can buy, or sell back, at any time. | `web/src/lib/market.ts`, `contracts/verity_market/src/math.rs` |
| **AI writer** | Each fact has a sentence for each confidence band (70%+ true, 30–70% disputed, under 30% doubtful). When a bet crosses a band, the page rewrites the sentence. With a NEAR AI key, a NEAR-hosted model writes the new sentence itself. | `web/src/lib/ai.ts` |
| **AI judge + disputes** | Anyone can ask for a ruling by posting a bond. The judge answers True, False, or "can't settle yet" (bond returned). Within the dispute window anyone can dispute with a bigger bond. A dispute goes to a panel of three different models, and the majority wins. | `web/src/lib/ai.ts`, `web/src/app/api/live/judge`, `contracts/verity_market/src/lib.rs` |
| **Any coin in** | NEAR Intents' 1Click API: quotes for Demo, real deposit addresses for Live, with refunds to the bettor's NEAR Intents balance if anything fails. | `web/src/lib/intents.ts`, `web/src/app/api/live/*` |
| **Contract** | USDC bets via `ft_transfer_call`, sell any time, on-chain price history, judge and panel roles, bonds, finalize, redeem, withdraw. One-call setup for many facts. | `contracts/verity_market` |
| **Both modes, one UI** | Every screen reads through one interface, backed by the browser (Demo) or the contract (Live). | `web/src/lib/book.ts` |

### Why "perpetual" markets still track the truth

A market that never ends has a known weakness. With nothing to pay out against, people may bet on what *others will believe*, not on what is *true*. Verity's answer is the Augur pattern, made cheap with AI:

- **Most facts never need settling.** The price is useful by itself, and you profit by being early and right, then selling when others catch up.
- **Any checkable fact can be forced to a final answer.** That threat ties prices to reality. If a price drifts away from the evidence, someone can post a bond, get a ruling, and collect.
- **Facts that can't be checked yet** (Mallory in 1924, octopus dreams) get "can't settle yet" from the judge. The market stays open, and the page honestly calls them disputed.
- **The judge can be challenged.** A dispute costs more than a request and goes to several independent models. Whoever is wrong loses their bond.

### Verifiable AI on NEAR

With `NEAR_AI_API_KEY` set, the writer and judges call [NEAR AI Cloud](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/). The default models are NEAR-hosted and run in TEEs (sealed hardware): Qwen 3.8 27B, GLM 5.3 Flash and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash.

After each answer, the server fetches `GET /v1/signature/{chat_id}` and checks that the signed text covers the SHA-256 of our exact request and response bytes. Each vote then shows *"Signed in a sealed computer ✓"*.

In Live mode the model names, verdicts and signer addresses are stored on-chain in the ruling's `evidence`.

## What's real and what's simulated

| Real | Simulated |
|---|---|
| Market math, identical in the app and the contract (tested in both) | Demo money: $1,000 of play money in `localStorage` |
| The contract, tested on NEAR testnet end to end | Demo coin transfers (the quote is real) |
| Live NEAR Intents quotes and deposit addresses | Demo's 48-hour dispute window (a button skips it) |
| NEAR AI writer, judge and panel, with signature checks, when a key is set | Without a key: prewritten sentences and rulings from `web/src/lib/content.ts` |
| | Demo price history before your first bet (generated, stable per fact) |

**Known limits:**
- The owner seeds every market (2 USDC each on mainnet). Pools are small, so one bet moves prices a lot.
- The judge and panel are trusted accounts. Their answers are signed by TEEs, but the contract doesn't verify the attestation on-chain yet. The judge key can only post rulings, not move money.
- The contract pays its own storage; a 0.10 USDC minimum bet limits spam.
- The wallet sign-in itself is tested on mainnet by a person, since it needs a real wallet.
